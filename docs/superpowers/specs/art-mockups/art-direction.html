Art direction — which mood?

Same Block-zone moment in three directions, drawn as vector shapes and snapped to the 270-wide pixel grid (exactly how the real sprites would be built). Below each phone: the kid + can at 3× so you can see the pixels.

A · Sunny Suburb

The placeholder grown up. Bright afternoon, no outlines, one soft shade tone per shape. Calm and clean; kid in a yellow hoodie.

B · Golden Hour Skip-Day

Late-afternoon "should be doing homework" vibe. Sunset sky, long shadows, warm rim light, lit windows. Kid with backpack + headphones. Coziest, most atmospheric.

C · Toon Outline

Saturday-morning cartoon. Dark plum outlines on everything, punchier colors, big-head kid in a backwards cap. Loudest, reads best at a glance during fast play.
